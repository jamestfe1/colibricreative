Meet Marina Ferreira

Marina practices systems thinking, orchestration, and digital transformation as one discipline: reading what a system actually does, tracing every process back to the people and hours it consumes, and connecting signals and teams so work runs without depending on any one person.

Colibri Creative is her space for changing the rules, information flows, and tools only as far as “enough”—so technology gives people back their time instead of claiming more of it, and work becomes lighter, fairer, and able to run on its own.
